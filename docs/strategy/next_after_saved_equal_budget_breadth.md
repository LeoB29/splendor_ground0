## Why fresh confirmation is the next decision

The completed result narrowly cleared the point-estimate hurdle. Its interval
is positive but does not establish a true gain of at least0.5pp. These data had
already shown a breadth benefit before we chose this experiment. Disjoint
selection/assessment inside each partition prevents one form of optimism, but
does not erase reuse of the positions or outcomes.

The64 partitions per root reduce dependence on the particular saved-world
split; they do not provide64 independent replications. A new study must test
the actual fixed decision procedure on new positions and world draws. Its
uncertainty may differ because it does not average64 overlapping partitions.
Keeping360 positions is a bounded investment choice, not a power guarantee.
Do not increase the panel until a favorable result appears.

This test asks whether eight candidates with less precision improve decision
quality at the four-candidate simulation budget. It does not yet ask whether
the cheaper version equals the strongest eight-by128 player or establishes
broader/human strength. The stalled repeatable learning loop remains a separate
research obstacle.

## One fixed three-arm comparison

Use the three unchanged original161 fits, greedy035 terminal continuations,
the mature observer-legal world law, canonical representatives and original
candidate-order ties. Retain the original candidate mixture: common first
three, original161 fourth, up to two additional anchor-ranked moves, and up to
two identity-uniform remaining moves. The failed new learners are not used.
Candidate generation keeps the original128-world convention and is common to
all arms; U's smaller allocation changes only which outcome cells it may read.

| Arm at full support | Candidates | Worlds per candidate | Continuations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Reference A |4|128|512|
| Uniform breadth U |8|64|512|
| Established expensive breadth T |8|128|1,024|

A and U provide the primary efficiency comparison. T is contemporaneous
context: it measures how much the lower-precision version leaves behind and
whether the established broader operator shows headroom on this new panel.
T is not a new learner, a selectable fallback winner or an equivalence target.

For canonical support G, m=min(G,4), n=min(G,8), B=128m. Give each U candidate
q=floor(B/n) worlds and give r=B mod n candidates one additional world using a
frozen identity-only priority. Thus G5/6/7 use102-or103,85-or86,73-or74 worlds
respectively. A uses128 worlds for each of its original m candidates. T uses128
for all n. For G<=4 all arms are identical and remain in the analysis. U and A
spend exactly the same continuation count at every root.

Collect128 common selection worlds for the n candidates. A may read only its
first m candidates; U may read only its assigned q/q+1 prefixes; T may read
the complete matrix. Compute each mean using its own actual count and exact
rational comparisons, with first original candidate winning ties. Simulating
the larger shared matrix for the experiment does not grant U access to the
unallocated outcomes. Independently check every arm's permitted cell accesses.

Persist all three choices before collecting128 disjoint assessment worlds.
Evaluate only their distinct selected actions, at most three, on those shared
assessment worlds. Never choose a new action from assessment results. There
is one deployment decision per arm per root, with no64-partition averaging.

## Cost and verification

At full support, shared selection costs8x128=1,024 continuations per root,
and assessing three distinct choices costs at most384. Maximum main collection
is360x1,408=506,880 continuations; the18-root probe adds at most25,344. Actual
assessment cost falls when arms choose the same action. There is no training.

Relevant observed eight-worker runs: original breadth collected599,936 main
continuations in7,940 seconds and audited in962 seconds; the specialist pilot
collected257,792 in2,829 seconds and audited in450 seconds. Different moves can
produce different game lengths. Scaling only those collection rates to this
ceiling gives approximately1.5-1.9 hours for main collection, with sources,
probe, checks and closure additional. Propose roughly2-4 hours of CPU execution
after preparation, subject to the18-root outcome-blind runtime/storage probe.
Equal branch budgets do not establish equal per-arm wall time, particularly
when experimental arms share simulations.

Use an eight-hour execution ceiling, five-GiB reserve, eight hidden workers and
one CPU numerical thread per worker. No visible terminals or recurring monitor.
Freeze the entire remaining-work forecast before main collection; an over-budget
forecast is a hold, not an automatic extension.

Apply docs/engineering/verification_policy.md: authenticate source/model/code
identities; check every actual source decision, proposal, legal input, candidate
budget, selection access, chosen action and statistic. Independently reconstruct
final paired statistics. Fully replay the bounded probe and frozen stress roots;
use the prescribed sampled continuation replay elsewhere on the mature engine.
Do not repeat full historical training or routine exhaustive main replay.
Report recorded versus replayed continuations and shared trusted primitives.

If authorized later, implement only in new files outside all frozen inventories.
No implementation, inference, game, rollout, statistical resampling, test,
training, launch or promotion was performed by this recommendation review.
