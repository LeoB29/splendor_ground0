**2. The strongest new diagnosis: the earlier oracle was only conditionally independent.**

Experiment 086's positive offline gain, +0.051199 normalized score margin with CI95 [+0.042185, +0.060546], survives new continuation-action randomness. It was not tested against fresh sampled worlds. Its implementation prepares the three determinizations, root refill outcomes, and opponent reply lists once. Discovery uses continuation values 0--3; evaluation uses values 4--15 from those same post-root states. The split is legitimate for continuation randomness but leaves world-sampling generalization untested. This is a missing validation axis, not evidence of outcome leakage. Maximizing over more actions can select actions that happened to look good on those three worlds. Every evaluation half inherits the same favorable worlds. Consequently, high discovery/evaluation correlation and low conditional regret can coexist with a substantial gameplay loss.

Belief-split regret was 5.83 times continuation-split regret. Experiment 059 reduced early regret with broader world coverage, but did not establish a reliable direct-label teacher. Neither result proves that world overfitting caused 134; they make it the most concrete unresolved causal explanation. These sampled worlds include deck/refill uncertainty as well as private-card possibilities: the finding does not automatically favor CFR. **3. Remaining hypotheses, ranked by current evidence and usefulness.**

1. **The search objective generalizes poorly beyond the samples and continuation policy used to construct it.** The missing independent-world axis above is directly testable. If its conditional gain survives fresh worlds, shallow planning followed by stochastic 035, truncated opponent responses, repeated replanning, and the resulting state distribution become stronger explanations. **5. The single next experiment: independent-world validation of frozen root choices.**

This is a proposed design for review, not a frozen protocol or implementation. Its question is: **does 086's advantage over the 084 action disappear when evaluation resamples worlds as well as later action randomness?**

- Use 60 existing 086 snapshots, ten per phase/seat cell and one per source game, chosen deterministically from identifiers without using payoff, winner, disagreement size, or effect sign. Preserve the original discovery-selected 084 and 086 semantic action keys, including cases where they coincide. This reuses known states with fresh evaluation randomness; it is a mechanism study, not fresh-population confirmation.
- Evaluate only those two fixed actions. Run four equal-budget blocks in each of two conditions: original three discovery worlds with fresh continuation randomness, and an independent three-world batch with fresh continuation randomness. Each block uses up to four legal 035 opponent replies and four stochastic-035 continuations per leaf. Maximum work is 60 x 2 conditions x 4 blocks x 2 actions x 3 worlds x 4 replies x 4 continuations = **46,080 terminal continuations**. No new source games are needed.
- Freeze disjoint world and rollout namespaces before evaluation. Couple randomness between the two candidate actions where legal. The fresh condition must independently resample full observer-legal worlds, including remaining deck orders; it must not preserve the discovery refill cards. Rebuild post-root states and legal reply lists in each world. Preserve a single minimizing reply across identical opponent information sets, using the existing grouped-resolution contract. Handle terminal roots and fewer-than-four replies explicitly; do not discard difficult worlds or carry old reply IDs across different refills.
- Resolve each three-world block separately, then average block estimates. Pooling the twelve fresh worlds before the nonlinear opponent minimum would change the operator and confound the independence test. Never reselect either root action using evaluation data. Both conditions use the deployed four-continuation block estimator; this is not an exact replication of 086's headline payoff estimator, which minimized after pooling twelve evaluation continuations. Its existing four-continuation evaluation halves are useful descriptive anchors, not new control evidence.
- Primary endpoint: fresh-world minus original-world change in the fixed 086-minus-084 normalized score-margin advantage. Estimate uncertainty at the source-game level, with the same game weighting in displayed means and intervals. A proposed material attenuation is 0.025 normalized margin, half a final prestige point and roughly half the previous 086 gain; freeze the threshold and precision analysis before collecting evidence. Report both absolute gains as well as their difference.
- Record rule-correct terminal WDL, card-count tiebreaks, action families, root counts, and existing-private-card status from the same trajectories. WDL comparisons are secondary checks of utility alignment; they do not turn stochastic-035 continuations or a sampled opponent minimum into an optimal-play oracle. Freeze exactly how margin-selected and WDL-selected opponent responses are reported.
- Require complete accounting, legal observations, resolved continuations, explicit deadlock handling, and an outcome-blind runtime preflight before a separately authorized launch. Do not add training, a new planner, candidate tuning, or a full-game arena to this diagnostic.

If the original-world condition reproduces a positive advantage and that advantage collapses materially under fresh worlds, the programme should prioritize independently validated action-value estimates and uncertainty under root expansion before learning from an oracle. If a positive advantage survives with enough precision to exclude material attenuation, selection overfitting becomes less persuasive; continuation-policy mismatch, limited horizon, and the changed gameplay distribution become the next crux. If the original-world control itself does not reproduce a positive advantage, report that the conditional gain was not reproduced under this estimator; do not attribute the failure specifically to fresh worlds. If intervals remain wide, report unresolved evidence. None of these outcomes rescues 086 or authorizes an automatic successor.

**6. What eventual superhuman evidence must look like.**

The positive foundations remain substantial: exact 084 beat 035 at 75.875%; the early AlphaZero loop compounded across two powered blocks; and additional AlphaZero search produced a real gain. These establish useful mechanisms, not a superhuman ceiling. The ten-game human calibration was 7--3 for one human and formally inconclusive. It is neither a population benchmark nor proof that the resolver cannot become superhuman.

Once the improvement signal is validated, judge learned components by the complete planner's strength at matched time, or preservation of strength at materially lower time. Raw-network improvement and complete search compression are optional. Require improvement that persists across successive generations and a league containing distinct policies, rather than only ancestors. Validate the rules against an independent implementation or audited rule cases. A final superhuman claim needs prospective play against multiple strong humans and independent bots under a declared move-time/hardware budget. These are programme milestones, not extra experiments to launch now.
