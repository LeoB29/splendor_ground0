Separately,160's original128-world planner scored65.5% against original32,

The successful learned32 planner has not faced it, and the learned proposal has

The next question is therefore: **does learning improve the strongest validated
teacher at its existing sampling budget?** It is possible that learned guidance
and additional sampling reinforce one another; it is also possible that their
benefits overlap or change with the positions reached. The answer is unmeasured.
Success would establish a stronger teacher in this direct matchup, not universal
dominance, a completed sequence of improving generations or superhuman strength.

## Why this can advance learning instead of becoming another tournament

Preserve each actual decision's lawful observation, proposals, model provenance,
full action-by-world outcomes, selected move and original trajectory identity.
Similar arenas produced about24,000 total decisions, roughly12,000 from the
candidate. That is a planning anchor, not a promised corpus size. These are
128-world targets on the new learned proposal support, obtained during the
strength experiment rather than requiring a separate full collection run.

Such records are useful development material, not automatically a clean training
experiment. The opponents act at different reached states; their records are
**not matched same-position labels from both teachers**. Later attribution of
supervision quality may require common-state relabeling. Future splits must keep
whole source seed pairs together, retain every fit and obey private-information
rules. Once arena records become training/development material, never reuse the
same games as fresh confirmation. Use new evaluation games and honest controls.

If the new teacher wins, the default following scientific question is one
controlled learning generation, evaluated through the model's proposal role in
the planner. Do not require another raw-ranked128 tournament, common-ancestor
confirmation or weak-opponent panel merely to postpone that step. Faster direct
policy play and teacher-label agreement cannot substitute for that deployment
endpoint;158 and161 already warn against such substitutions.

If the comparison is negative or inconclusive, retain the established learned32
result and do not force a new high-budget training cycle or automatically extend
the arena. Reconsider cheaper controlled proposal learning or how sampling and
candidate quality interact. A failed matchup does not disprove those approaches.

## Strongest alternative: train from existing records now

This is credible and cheaper in data collection. A teacher need not beat every
earlier teacher before its examples can help.162 also does not rule out learning
on the newly expanded proposal support: it used the old support and a different
downstream deployment question.

However, a naive update from current32-world records changes support, visited
positions and label sampling precision relative to161's128-world supervision.
Another weak update would be difficult to diagnose. A controlled version needs
matched extra old-data training, an unchanged161 reference, and evaluation inside
the planner. At the established sample size, two such32-world planner comparisons
would mean about804 games; cheap fitting alone understates the total cost. A
staged design could reduce that cost but adds a separate predeclared decision.

One equal128 study has a clearer immediate question and supplies higher-budget
examples for the subsequent learning experiment. This is a judgment about likely
information per total compute, not a theorem that proving teacher superiority
must always precede training.

## Cost, verification and the engineering work

Use the owner-approved [verification policy](../engineering/verification_policy.md):
check every actual game, decision, proposal, lawful world and arithmetic record;
replay all candidates in eight hash-selected worlds out of128 at every root,
plus the fixed full-root stress subset. Validate the new budget adapter and audit
sampling on bounded fully checked fixtures. Disclose sampled inner replay rather
than claiming every continuation was independently reconstructed.

Allow roughly **70--90 hours, three to four days of execution**, plus preparation,
with the existing serial machinery. This is provisional until the operational
probe.160's128-versus32 collection took41.15h; scaling its combined allowance
from160 to256 worlds gives about65.8h collection before small schedule differences.
The latest32-versus32 collection took15.82h, whose fourfold anchor is63.3h.
Sampled audits, stress coverage, probe and overhead remain additional costs;
the new policy's savings are not yet measured. The old five-to-six-day estimate
assumed full replay of every continuation and no longer describes this design.

The prepared [batching benchmark](../engineering/batching_benchmark_readiness.md)
is a useful bounded engineering job once current workers are free. Its full
equivalence stage precedes its timing stage. It need not become an indefinite
prerequisite for this scientific study. A failure or negligible speed benefit
leaves the serial design available. A positive result does not silently enable
batching in production:128-world workloads and integration would still need
appropriate checks before a new study is frozen. Assume no speedup in this budget.

This recommendation does not launch either benchmark stage or the128 study.
Keep hidden workers, durable launches and monitoring-off preferences. Use new
implementation files outside frozen inventories and preserve all existing work.

## Other alternatives

- Learned32 versus original128 would test practical compute efficiency, at a
  lower cost. Prefer it if decision latency becomes the main objective. A loss
  would still leave the128 combination unresolved; an inconclusive result is
  not evidence of equal strength or a fourfold speed advantage.
- A new single-decision screen is cheaper, but cannot establish repeated
  full-game transfer. Conditional proposal quality already has positive evidence;
  another screen now risks delaying the consequential high-budget question.
- Broader independent opponents remain necessary for eventual superhuman claims.
  The available cheap local panel is not a substitute for a strong external
  benchmark, and its162 result did not establish broad learning progress.
- More training passes, replacing simulated policies, or combining adaptive
  sampling are not established remedies. Keep them out of this comparison.
