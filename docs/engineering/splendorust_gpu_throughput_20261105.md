decisions may exceed the previous 9-second internal / 10-second outer allowance.

and 75-second independent outer safeguard**, including decoding, queueing, native
search, inference, response observation and validation. An expired safeguard
stops the affected work; it does not prove the process was literally stuck.
No allowance is restarted inside a decision or extended after observing results.

profile remains authenticated, but raw result wrapper identities explicitly name
the new policy. Historical comparison may normalize only that validated wrapper
identity to its source identifier. All action, trace, search-work, root output,
