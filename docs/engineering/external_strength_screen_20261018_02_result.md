# Stronger external opponent: accepted 60-game screen, 18 October 2026

The adapted SplendoRust Entity PUCT16 player qualified on the corrected rules and beat raw035 **58 wins to 2 losses**, with no draws, forfeits or timeouts. It won 29 of 30 games from each seat. This establishes a useful, substantially stronger external opponent for raw035 at the one-second deployment allowance. It does not establish its strength against our four- or eight-candidate planners, whose historical results used the old rules.

**Recommended next step:** qualify our four-candidate/128-world planner under the corrected rules, retaining all three prescribed model fits and the existing success criteria. It is the first practical research candidate because the historical four-candidate qualification completed all 26 requests within 120 seconds, while eight candidates timed out on eight requests; neither completed the one-second qualification. Those historical timings and decisions must be rechecked after the rule correction. This recommendation does not promote the four-candidate planner above the historically stronger eight-candidate planner or silently substitute it.

Once corrected-rules correctness and timing are established, freeze the exact local player and a matched thinking-time allowance for both sides, then requalify the external profile at that allowance before designing the next head-to-head. Giving our planner 120 seconds against this fixed one-second external profile would answer a different, unequal-compute question. The present result justifies retaining SplendoRust as a serious external control; it does not by itself authorize a 402-game benchmark, prove elite strength or determine the next study's sample size.

The larger independent-data study remains **deferred** until storage and evidence justify its investment. No model training, new data collection or further games were launched after this screen.
