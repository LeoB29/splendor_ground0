## Question and proposed programme

The accepted data-size study found better held-out policy fit with more games,
including a smaller positive512-to1024-game increment. This supports testing a
larger independent corpus; it does not yet establish a playing-strength gain or
predict the benefit of another doubling accurately. Search-repeatability JSD and
policy teacher-gap cross-entropy are different measurements and must not be
subtracted or compared as a calibrated fraction of one noise floor.

Collect3,072 fresh training games and256 fresh sealed assessment games. Combine
the new training games with the existing accepted1,024 training games, giving
4,096 independent training games. The old128 held-out games remain historical
diagnostics, not newly independent assessment. The new256 are stored as two
128-game partitions; prospectively pool them for the primary offline comparison.
Neither partition is used to choose training duration, targets or checkpoints.

The teacher is original historical EF8A,512 total simulations, three hidden
worlds, cpuct0.4, depth64 and float32. Reuse the accepted CPU8 collector: eight
concurrent search clients and the unchanged qualified inference service. Keep
the full legal root-visit policy, original root-value evidence, official
actor-oriented terminal outcomes, all callbacks and corrected snapshots.
No target or game is replaced following a failure. No live-game match is part
of this collection job, and no scientific model is fitted or promoted.

The prepared follow-on training design compares two4,096-game one-pass fits
with two1,024-game controls matched for the exact number of record presentations,
batch sizes and optimizer updates. Controls cycle deterministic shuffled passes,
truncated to the same total presentations as the full new corpus. This separates
extra independent information from extra optimization. All four start from
pristine EF8A, using the accepted mixed search/outcome value target and unchanged
one-pass numerical recipe. Existing1,024-game one-pass checkpoints provide a
separately labelled practical baseline. Commit all endpoints before predictions
on the new256 assessment games. Use whole-game paired uncertainty, policy gap
closure and value drift; a fresh live strength test would be separately frozen.
These training/evaluation stages are a prepared follow-on, not released by this
collection-only owner. The controlled schedule must be qualified before fitting.
