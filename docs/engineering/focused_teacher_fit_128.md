## Interpretation and boundaries

Low old-label regret with recovered independent utility demonstrates local
acquisition under focused exposure. Weak validation transfer then motivates a
controlled generalization investigation; it does not prove that more ordinary
epochs would solve the original problem. Failure to fit even this small set
motivates inspecting optimization, encoding and capacity jointly, without claiming
the architecture is impossible. Lower loss without useful choices is insufficient;
inspect the consequential remaining errors. No model promotion follows.
