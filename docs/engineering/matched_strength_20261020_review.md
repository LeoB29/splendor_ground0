# Prospective matched-strength review

This review covers the new sixty-game plan and harness prepared on 20–21 October
2026. It does not contain a strength result or permission to launch. The larger
independent-data study remains deferred.

Separate reviewers examined (1) the explicit context adapters, clock and runner,
(2) the independent game/receipt/replay analyzer, and (3) statistics, measured
resource pricing and final completion handling. The parent reviewed their
interfaces, scientific scope and protocol. Only new versioned files are changed;
the accepted qualified players, operational evidence and all earlier failures
remain immutable inputs.

The sample-size recommendation explicitly prices the original 402-game roadmap
and the smaller sixty-game screen. The owner asked for our recommendation; the
chosen fixed sample is thirty fresh pairs, ten per fit, with no conditional
extension. A 72-hour ceiling, eighteen-hour post-game reserve, eight-GiB evidence
ceiling and bounded RAM waiting are fixed before outcomes. The cost estimate
uses operational timing and replay records, not prior matchup wins or losses.

new explicit context admission, separate private referee and public search
randomness, complete swapped-seat scheduling, all-three-fit allocation, original
120-second trusted receive clock, preserved timeout forfeits, complete fixed-
sample acceptance, deterministic continuation selection, failed-case retention,
