## Timing admission

Both quotas must independently satisfy all conditions on the complete panel:

- All 192 decisions complete correctly within the unchanged one-second clock.
- Every isolated-placement decision arrives before 700 ms.
- Isolated mean arrival time is at most 105% of the original-placement mean.
- Isolated 95th-percentile arrival is at most 25 ms above the original placement.

Percentiles use linear interpolation. Use trusted receipt times without any
subtraction. The 700 ms threshold is an engineering margin, not a timeout:
an accepted 750 ms move is still a completed move, even though it fails this
study's admission. A negative gate on complete correct data is a verified
negative qualification, not a retroactive player loss.

This failure-enriched, dependent panel is not a population reliability estimate.
If the old slow behavior does not recur, passing establishes feasibility and
nonregression under the observed conditions; it does not prove a causal cure.
